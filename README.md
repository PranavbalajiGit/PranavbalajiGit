## 👨‍💻 Executive Summary & Engineering Profile

I am **PRANAV BALAJI P MA**, a **Software Engineer & Applied AI / Systems Enthusiast** dedicated to engineering high-performance distributed architectures and production-grade Generative AI applications. My core expertise sits at the intersection of **scalable backend systems**, **real-time event streaming**, and **modern AI model integration**.

- 🏗️ **Distributed Systems & Backend Architecture:** Experienced in architecting robust microservices, resilient asynchronous workflows, and high-throughput RESTful / WebSocket backends using Node.js, Express, Next.js, and Java.
- 🧠 **Applied AI & Generative AI Systems:** Engineering production-ready GenAI platforms, prompt pipelines, and multi-tenant AI SaaS workflows, alongside active research into autonomous LLM agents and RAG architectures.
- ⚡ **High-Concurrency Real-Time Infrastructure:** Proven background in designing low-latency communication systems (WebSockets, WebRTC, Stream SDKs) supporting live peer-to-peer video streaming, presence tracking, and synchronized state.
- 🔒 **Production Rigor & DevOps:** Dedicated to clean architecture, automated CI/CD pipelines, containerized deployments with Docker, and battle-tested authentication architectures (JWT HTTP-only, OAuth2).
